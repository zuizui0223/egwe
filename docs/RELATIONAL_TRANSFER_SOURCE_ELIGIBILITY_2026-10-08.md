## Decision

**0/3** reviewed programmes pass the full natural H-R admission gate. This is a **coverage/identifiability count**, not a sample-size-based ecological null, not evidence against H-R, and not a new biological result.

## Guard against accidental evidence inflation

- The Honshu–Izu 40 networks are not 40 independent future genetic trajectories.
- *Oenothera* fruit rows are nested within 23 maternal plants and cannot be pooled as independent population-year outcomes.
- The *Eschscholzia* pan-trap state is not observed effective pollen transfer; the F metadata inconsistency remains a preregistered STOP.
- Combining geography/functional data from one study with genetics from another does not recover patchwise covariance.
- The NEE first natural strongest-refuge transfer remains a separate locked non-positive portability test, not an eligible H-R test.

## Next source-search criterion

Search specifically for a *single* archived longitudinal field experiment with matched local pollinator/interaction measurements, plant reproductive trait or parentage/genotypes, repeated population/site units, and subsequent seed set/offspring recruitment. Before opening outcomes, inspect the raw archive schema, sample/time alignment, and independent validation units. If no such archive is found, stop at **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE** rather than tuning proxies or retrospectively merging incompatible sources.
