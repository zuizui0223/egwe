# Cross-closure v1 locked-trajectory descriptive QA — 2026-11-17

**Status:** post-outcome diagnostic only; no prospective rerun, no confirmatory analysis. This work is owned by EGWE, which generated the finite model, not by the EGWEE natural-data evidence lane.

Original prospective run: [44012157673](https://github.com/zuizui0223/egwe/actions/runs/44012157673), artifact [14296518638](https://github.com/zuizui0223/egwe/actions/runs/44012157673/artifacts/14296518638). The fixed, preregistered D outcome and its six-block CIs remain **unresolved** in both closures. This diagnostic cannot rescue that result.

The new script downloads **the exact existing completed artifact**, checks the source's per-file SHA256 manifest and every unique 2×2×2×2×6×160 trajectory key, then reproduces original cell event rates and primary D **without new simulation or changing denominators**. For each original condition it reports AA/RR first-loss incidence, early loss, later reappearance, final synthetic high-trait mass and model opportunity (latter readouts remain descriptive). It will also identify event-incidence saturation, which is not equivalent to a biological absence of effect.

Three limitations remain decisive. First, the two closures use different semantics for `q`: in A it is an aggregate sigmoid support state, in B a stochastic visit-encounter fraction. Second, model B changes both partner supply and visitation dynamics, so any difference cannot be attributed uniquely to partner availability. Third, the primary first all-patch focal-trait absence may be followed by re-expression; it is **not** demographic extinction or permanent function loss.

Relevant prior art already distinguishes visitation frequency, pollen delivery, pollen quality and compatibility (Aizen & Harder 2007 DOI 10.1890/06-1017; Young & Pickup 2010 DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2664.2010.01798.x; recent synthesis DOI 10.1146/annurev-environ-121224-092446). The scientific challenge is not to rediscover this distinction but to test **conditional predictive sufficiency of a biologically matched state, across independently replicated systems**.

This evidence check adds no new flagship manuscript Results, does not change any old frozen scientific estimate, and does not claim a new universal eco-genetic rule.
