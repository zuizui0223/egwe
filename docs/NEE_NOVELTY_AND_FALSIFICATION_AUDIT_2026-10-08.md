## 2. What the current evidence actually establishes

**Question 1: state separation.** Under the declared fixed-area projection and canonical threshold geometry, 1,037/1,037 eligible one-patch sources lose *potential* high-trait viability on subdivision while nearly all retain realised trait occupancy at generation 30. The effect is strong **within the designed model**, but the discrete viability switch is structurally tied to the imposed area-scaled feedback. Do not treat 1,037 replicated projections as 1,037 independent discoveries of the threshold or as empirical fragmentation observations. The non-identical trajectories of interaction, local effective size and trait mass are the biologically interpretable part of the contrast. Evidence: EGC canonical gradient pinned by the flagship source manifest.

**Question 2: hidden organization.** Matched-marginal aligned/reversed states differ in exact next-q transition by up to 0.2543 at the same mean support; covariance changes support variance 49-fold. This is a valid constructive counterexample to the sufficiency of the **specified** coarse state, not proof that any one empirical covariance universally controls loss. The original later-risk contrasts (+5.33 and +5.20 pp) remain conditional on that fixed experimental setting. Evidence: flagship article, relational mechanism decomposition and associated frozen artifacts.

**Causal operator resolution.** The isolated q-dependent allele-selection edge changed the generation-40 RR–AA difference by +6.883 pp (95% CI +5.800,+7.967) in the prospectively fixed 6,000-pair intervention. Recruitment exactly contracts the specified trait–allele mismatch by 50% under the chosen kernel weight; direct support feedback contracts the specified q–bundle mismatch by 40%; the density-to-q edge strongly amplifies loss. These identities and interventions diagnose **roles of model operators**, not universal natural laws. The subsequent fresh route-duration primary was *negative* for prolonging above-switch refuge duration; the same-ensemble endpoint benefit is post hoc and cannot replace that primary.

**Early warning and future-fate ranking.** Diversity erosion preceded 35/35 and 33/33 observed losses but fired in 48/48 and 49/49 non-events: six binary rules have specificity 0 and AUC 0.5. The exact one-step route-sign marker was also saturated for later fate. A *different, continuous* max-patch route margin, prospectively tested in 12,000 new trajectories, had seed-block mean AUC 0.92734 and incremental paired AUC +0.02135 [0.01770,0.02501] against co-timed max q. These are not mutually contradictory: the binary rule discards reserve depth, while the continuous score retains it. The locked orientation of H_alpha gave AUC 0.23253 and was not flipped post hoc.

## 3. Generality bottlenecks with evidence status

1. **Closure dependence.** The operator weights, q*=0.625 switch, density feedback and forcing schedule are specified features of the simulator. Strong operator DIDs within it do not estimate natural causal effect sizes.
2. **Last-refuge endpoint advantage.** The holdout used zero migration, two fixed AA/RR initial templates, an all-patch trait-loss endpoint and a max-over-patches score. The max operation has a structural relationship to that endpoint. The confirmatory incremental claim is only versus **co-timed max q**; exploratory max(S) and max(dq) comparisons were post hoc.
3. **Natural bridge unresolved.** The separate first natural prospective transfer did not detect added strongest-refuge information after contemporaneous marginals (delta NLL M1–M0 = -0.0003211; species-bootstrap 95% CI [-0.0009825,+0.0003422]). The five-cluster natural state-separation synthesis is influence- and covariance-dependent. Neither result validates the q-dependent selection, recruitment, recoupling or density operator.
4. **Ecological geography is not a mechanism.** Urban local pollination failure and island movement compensation are distinct causal routes; no measured urban–island equivalence or convergence has been demonstrated. A location label must not substitute for effective interaction, successful mating, process-specific movement or cohort turnover.

## 4. The ecological falsifier for the next independent evidence route

The model's strongest **portable hypothesis**, rather than its numeric threshold, is:

> Among fragmented populations with comparable census, interaction, trait and genetic **marginals**, patchwise *co-location* of interaction and trait/genetic support, together with process-specific sorting, recruitment and movement, can explain differences in future realised function.

A prospective natural test would compare future reproductive/functional outcomes across held-out sites or time windows using (i) co-timed marginals alone, (ii) those marginals plus patch-level association and (iii) measured process-specific recruitment or effective movement. If held-out fate cannot be improved by relational/process information, the proposed natural generalization is **not supported**, regardless of simulation AUC. A null may also reflect unreliable measurement of interaction, mating or cohorts; the interpretation must distinguish these possibilities *before* outcome inspection. This is a hypothesis and design boundary, **not** a claim that such a prospective natural test has been run.

The next simulator transport test, if pursued, must be a separate prospectively declared extension that changes initial-state and movement/forcing regimes without re-tuning the frozen flagship evidence; a favourable re-analysis of the same twelve seed blocks cannot establish cross-closure generality.

## 5. Editorial decision

**Safe central claim:** `In one declared finite eco-genetic closure, spatial organization and opposing life-cycle operators generate transition and fate differences hidden by matched marginals; continuous strongest-local reserve adds conditional fate-ranking information beyond co-timed maximum interaction, whereas early diversity erosion does not discriminate.`

**Do not claim:** newly discovered functional extinction before demographic loss; universal genome-based early warning; proven real-world urban–island equivalence; a universal 'eco-genetic closure' law; portable AUC 0.927; an independently confirmed mechanistic natural rescue route.

**Editorial risk remains:** *Nature Ecology & Evolution* may regard the results as an internally rigorous but biologically unvalidated model demonstration. Clearer wording and a shorter letter improve honesty and readability; **they do not substitute for external validation**. The active manuscript should remain a single NEE lane with its explicit natural null, and any new evidence route must be separated from existing scientific locks.
