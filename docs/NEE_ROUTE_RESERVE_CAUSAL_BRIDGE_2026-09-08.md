# NEE route-reserve causal bridge

Status: integration ledger for the clean current-main recovery of previously isolated prospective evidence.

## Purpose

The flagship manuscript already contained the focused q-dependent allele-sorting proof and the independent continuous last-refuge warning holdout. Two upstream prospective evidence layers were still stranded on old branches:

1. the exact operator-balance route-margin theorem plus its fresh 12,000-trajectory long-horizon boundary test;
2. the fresh 24,000-trajectory sorting–headroom follow-up that prospectively links the resolved sorting edge to continuous strongest-refuge reserve.

This bridge materializes those frozen protocols/results on current main without reopening simulations, changing seeds, tuning thresholds or reinterpreting natural systems as validation data.

## Recovered evidence chain

`matched-marginal covariance -> q-dependent allele sorting -> continuous last-refuge reserve -> later functional fate`

with two important boundaries:

- direct eco-genetic feedback is a one-step recoupling operator, but its fresh route-duration test resolves **against** generic above-switch refuge extension;
- exact route-margin sign and perfect temporal precedence do **not** guarantee later-fate discrimination, whereas an earlier continuous strongest-local margin does.
