# Venue rerouting decision — 2026-11-07

**Decision:** Prepare the single active eco-genetic fragmentation flagship for **Theoretical Ecology** as a **regular research article**, replacing the previous **Nature Ecology & Evolution** target without altering the closed scientific evidence.

## Why this journal
*Theoretical Ecology* explicitly accepts mathematical, computational and conceptual approaches that improve ecological understanding. The paper's strongest evidence is analytic and simulation-based, not a field-validated universal early-warning predictor. The new target is therefore a better match to the existing evidence ceiling. It does not guarantee acceptance. The second-choice fit is *Ecological Modelling*, subject to claim-overlap controls with the separately developed operator-portability Short Communication.

## Preserved scientific spine
Q1: Fixed-area fragmentation separates potential trait viability, realized occupancy, interaction, local effective size and functional support.

Q2: Matching conventional state marginals cannot guarantee matching transitions. Local interaction-dependent allele sorting, recruitment-mediated mismatch buffering, direct feedback recoupling and density amplification determine how contrasting states evolve. A continuous strongest-local eco-genetic reserve predicts later functional loss better than co-timed maximum interaction state within a prospectively locked finite-model holdout.

Do not discard: (i) the positive +6.883 percentage-point sorting DID, (ii) the AUC 0.92734 and +0.02135 increment, (iii) the 0-specificity binary warning result, or (iv) the directionally inverted H_alpha result. None of these numerical values is a natural-system estimate.
