# Theoretical Ecology — nearest-neighbor novelty and claim boundary (2026-11-07)

**Status:** literature-positioning and editorial revision only. **No newly run simulation, theorem, seed, endpoint, sensitivity analysis, figure, biological parameter or empirical validation.**

## Publication claim in one sentence

In a declared finite eco-genetic closure, matched ecological/genetic marginals can conceal different next transitions and later functional futures; prospective operator deletions separate **interaction-dependent allele sorting, recruitment mismatch buffering, direct eco-genetic recoupling and density collapse amplification**, while a continuous strongest-local *joint-state* reserve incrementally ranks subsequent trait loss beyond the co-timed strongest interaction state.

The novelty is the **linked mechanism-and-forecast audit within this closure**, not the existence of spatial covariance, feedback, potential interactions, local refuges or informative warning nodes per se.

## Positive evidence retained intact

1. **State separation:** 1,037/1,037 supported one-patch sources lose potential high-trait viability under tested subdivision while realised high-trait occupancy remains ~99.6–100% at generation 30. **Important limitation:** shared area-scaled feedback fixes the sharp threshold; 1,037 are *not* independent model closures.
2. **Transition insufficiency:** identical prespecified marginal summaries can hide a 0.2543 next-q difference. It falsifies sufficiency **of that particular vector**, not all conceivable low-dimensional states.
3. **Process identification:** q-dependent selection edge DID +6.883 pp, replicated in a separate 24,000-trajectory follow-up; recruitment and direct feedback produce algebraic mismatch reduction under chosen coefficients; density acts as an amplification/failure gate. Some full-feedback route-duration expectations fail.
4. **Discriminative monitoring:** six early genetic-diversity thresholds fire in all event and all non-event trajectories (binary specificity 0; AUC 0.5); a continuous max-patch margin improves rankings by +0.02135 AUC over co-timed max q.
5. **Scientific limits:** the max-over-patches score is structurally aligned with an all-patch-loss endpoint and was tested at zero migration in two initial arrangements. The first natural prospective transfer **did not** establish incremental predictive benefit; no natural universal 0.927 AUC, common urban/island regime, or general eco-genetic law was demonstrated.

## Reviewer-robust lead

> Prior work shows that spatial averages can be inadequate, eco-evolutionary feedback can govern extinction, potential interaction capacity can exceed realized networks, and selected nodes can improve warning. We ask the narrower mechanistic question: once ecological and genetic marginal summaries are held fixed, which **life-cycle transitions** turn hidden local association into functional loss, which transitions repair that mismatch, and does a joint-state local reserve convey independently held-out fate information beyond the strongest interaction state alone?

This claim is backed by the manuscript's matched-state example, prospective single-edge intervention and independent holdout. It is the defensible central novelty for *Theoretical Ecology*.
