# Theoretical Ecology novelty stress test — prior-art boundary and decisive next experiment
## Independent critical literature audit, 2026-11-17

**Status:** Literature and claim audit, not a new simulation result, prior approval, manuscript replacement, journal submission, or guarantee of originality. This document supplements the earlier `THEORETICAL_ECOLOGY_NEAREST_NEIGHBOR_2026-11-07.md` and changes no frozen endpoint, seed, operator or original hypothesis label.

## Executive verdict

The generic lead **"fragmentation separates biological layers; local cross-layer coupling governs functional fate"** is **not a sufficiently new general ecological principle** on its own. Spatial covariance, limitations of averaged-state representations, eco-evolutionary feedback, functional loss before population extinction, stochastic early-warning failures, and targeted spatial warning have independent prior art.

**Defensible narrower contribution:** Within **one declared finite eco-genetic life cycle**, the study links a matched-marginal one-step counterexample to **prospectively locked single-edge interventions** that separate interaction-conditioned allele sorting from recruitment mismatch buffering, direct recoupling and density-driven failure amplification, then checks an independent, continuous strongest-refuge holdout against a competitive co-timed maximum-q comparator. This is a specific mechanism-resolving study, **not** the discovery that ecological/genetic alignment or spatial heterogeneity matters in general.

## What the frozen results genuinely support

1. **Q1, closure-specific:** for a single family of patch-area-scaled finite models, 1,037/1,037 supported one-patch sources lost potential high-trait viability upon equal-area subdivision, while realized occupancy stayed near complete. This is not an independent component test of patch area vs exchange or a general ecological threshold.
2. **Constructive state insufficiency:** a chosen coarse summary vector mapped two full states onto the same summary while their exact next interaction field differed by up to 0.2543. This refutes sufficiency **only of this chosen summary vector** under the declared map. A conditional +5.33/+5.20 pp future-risk contrast appeared in one locked propagation experiment but the later full-feedback directional alignment contrasts were not universally stable.
3. **Strongest mechanistic evidence:** the prospectively locked q-dependent **allele-selection edge** yielded a generation-40 arrangement DID of **+6.883 pp**, 95% CI **[+5.800,+7.967]**; a separate fresh ensemble replicated the conditional operator effect. Recruitment and direct support have exact mismatch-repair identities **because of the model's defined weights**, and the density-edge experiment identified a strong collapse amplification pathway. These are valid finite-closure causal contrasts, not cross-model ecological laws.
4. **Prediction boundary:** all six genetic diversity warning rules had specificity zero; continuous strongest-local route margin AUC **0.92734**, versus co-timed maximum-q **0.90598**, gives paired ΔAUC **+0.02135** (95% CI [+0.01770,+0.02501]) in one frozen two-arrangement, zero-migration, all-patch-loss holdout. A max-over-patches predictor is structurally matched to the all-patch-loss endpoint; high absolute AUC is not portable.
5. **Critical failed natural transfer:** the first prospectively locked natural stronger-local-refuge evaluation showed **no confirmed incremental future occupancy prediction** after co-timed marginals (ΔNLL -0.0003211; species-block CI crossing zero). The natural multicase synthesis remains source-dependence-sensitive and cannot validate the finite operators.

## Where a meaningful next scientific advance could exist

The unresolved mechanistic question is **not** "do local arrangements matter?" The stricter question is:

> **When do ecological–genetic spatial mismatches become persistent functional-loss risk, and when are the same mismatches repaired? Can that sign/boundary be predicted from independently measured selection, recruitment and interaction feedback processes across more than one ecological closure?**

This is a **new hypothesis/test proposal**, not an observed general law. The current single-edge DID is a plausible seed for it. The decisive advance would be an **a priori regime boundary or sign prediction** in terms of sorting vs repair strength, tested out of sample, **not** further post-hoc extraction of positives from the same finite trajectories.

### Prospective scientific gate (new extension only; DO NOT alter frozen main evidence)

- Freeze a competing pair of update mechanisms **before** outcomes: (A) current allele-frequency/aggregate interaction model and (B) a non-equivalent, biologically defensible explicit interaction/partner-availability and effective recruitment formulation. Avoid calling allele mixing individual dispersal.
- Fix all starting marginal summaries and vary the **cross-layer arrangement** under matched area/density and controlled connectivity separately. Keep primary endpoint realized all-patch high-trait loss and one separate direct reproductive-function endpoint; define horizon and censored trajectories **before** looking.
- Predeclare a mechanism-based **sign prediction or threshold region** for the AA–RR differential, then preregister held-out parameter families, new master seeds, no outcome-driven region search, and a close competitor that uses the same marginals plus generic spatial covariance. A positive result must exceed a meaningful increment rather than merely p<0.05.
- Distinguish an alternative-closure **falsification** from the existing finite-closure success. If the directional sign or joint-state predictive increment fails under B, report a **nonportable operator-boundary** rather than claiming generality.
- For natural relevance, require synchronized site × patch × cohort observations of realized effective interaction, trait, compatible mating and recruitment; do not use the first natural null as validation or reselect its endpoint.

**Decision gate:** Until an independent cross-closure/held-out test is passed, avoid titles/abstracts such as "a new general law of eco-genetic warning", "fragmentation universally causes...", or "gene–interaction alignment determines natural fate." A model-specific mechanistic contribution remains potentially publishable in *Theoretical Ecology*, but its broad-concept novelty should not be overstated.
