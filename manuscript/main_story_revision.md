## Two load-bearing claims

### 1. State sufficiency requires joint representation

The constructive aligned/anti-aligned pair is the central model result. Common census, interaction, allele-frequency and trait marginals, `H_alpha`, `H_gamma` and `F_ST` can be identical while patchwise cross-layer alignment changes the exact next transition by `0.2543`. The fixed long-horizon comparison does not establish a directional loss-risk effect and remains explicitly bounded.

### 2. Empirical state claims require measurement and representation gates

Natural analyses follow one sequence:

`candidate-state adequacy -> information-preserving representation -> residual origin/history test`.

Honshu–Izu and Zurich illustrate residual-context redundancy after partial states; the independent Toronto replication now reaches the same residual-context stage and shows no held-out gain from adding urban cover and green-space edge density after its locked partial pollination/floral state. *Oenothera* illustrates a missing mating-connectivity coordinate; *Eschscholzia* illustrates an inadequate availability proxy plus a primary metadata non-identifiability; *Campanula* illustrates loss of mechanistic distinction through preprocessing.

Mallorca carob adds the complementary earlier-gate result. Its source workbook retained direct pollinator abundance, fruit production and landscape/management context at orchard scale, but two source-deposited `PolinAbun` representations were non-identical. Both were therefore frozen as mandatory before outcome fitting. In the one-shot leave-one-orchard-out NB2 test, neither representation earned reproducible predictive information for fruit production: embedded `Delta NLL=-0.10195`, 95% CI `[-3.12202,+3.61919]`; joined `-0.09919`, `[-3.14415,+3.66453]`. The B1 measurement-adequacy gate failed, so residual-context B2 was not opened.

This Toronto–carob contrast sharpens the empirical rule. Upstream context can only be tested for redundancy after the candidate process state itself has demonstrated endpoint-relevant predictive adequacy in a preserved representation. A direct urban-versus-island convergence test remains **not identifiable from the current Honshu–Izu and Zurich archives**, and Toronto plus carob do not repair that identifiability boundary merely because one urban system reaches the residual-context gate and one island system contains nominally analogous variables. This remains a **design boundary, not evidence against convergence**.

The stronger cross-origin hypothesis is therefore retained prospectively only under four additional requirements: the same biological state coordinates, the same direct functional response, prior endpoint-relevant adequacy of the candidate process state, and independent replication of origin apart from study identity. The minimum identifiable design is at least two independently measured systems per origin or one coordinated multi-origin protocol, with whole-system transfer validation.
