## Figure 1 — Evidence architecture: replication versus operator substitution

**Purpose:** prevent over-counting of evidence and make the inferential design visually explicit.

**Panel A — historical reference.** Historical seeds `20290410–20290414` with two shared reference conditions: no connectivity and allele-only `m=.10`.

**Panel B — independent replication.** Phase U repeats the allele-only contrast on new seeds `20291010–20291014`. This is the only independent fresh replication.

**Panel C — process substitutions.** Phase R and Phase S branch from the same historical reference observations, adding whole-individual `d=.10` and pollen-only `g=.20`, respectively. A brace or shared-node graphic must show that R/S are **not independent baseline replications**.

**Bottom line:** one independent replication + two shared-reference operator substitutions.
