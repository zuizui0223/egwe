## Positioning boundary

These papers already establish that connectivity is multiply defined, process-dependent and difficult to transport across ecological/evolutionary contexts. The novelty of the present finite-model note must therefore **not** be framed as discovering that “connectivity has different meanings.” The distinct contribution is a controlled portability audit: one historical allele-frequency-mixing pattern is subjected to one fresh independent seed replication and two prospectively declared process substitutions, showing that a stochastic result attached to a scalar connectivity parameter cannot be inherited by whole-individual or pollen-only operators merely because the parameter is called connectivity.
