# NEE flagship display plan — four-operator mechanism

## Principle

The main figures carry only exact mathematics and locked finite-model evidence. Natural systems remain Introduction/Discussion projections and receive no main-data panel.

## Figure 1 — From fragmentation to causal pathway balance

Define the manuscript in one view:

- fragmentation separates persistence from functional support;
- matched conventional marginals can hide transition-relevant cross-layer covariance;
- four life-cycle roles determine the later route:
  1. **sorting** — local q-dependent allele selection;
  2. **trait–allele buffering** — allele-linked recruitment;
  3. **interaction–bundle recoupling** — direct T/G -> q support feedback;
  4. **failure gate** — density -> q feedback;
- early marginal erosion need not discriminate which route is dominating.

Central synthesis:

`cross-layer covariance -> sorting <-> (trait–allele buffering + interaction–bundle recoupling) -> density-feedback collapse gate`

Footer: landscape labels, alignment scores and the model-specific algebraic weights are not universal mechanisms.

### Panel C — two mismatch-restoring processes

#### C1 Recruitment buffering

For resident high-trait mass `m` and high-allele frequency `p`:

`r=(m+p)/2` under locked `h=.5`.

Thus `|r-p|=.5|m-p|`: trait–allele mismatch contracts **50%** before selection.

Locked deletion:
- g20 DID **-9.00 pp** `[-13.29,-4.71]`;
- g40 DID **-8.33 pp** `[-12.53,-4.14]`.

#### C2 Direct feedback recoupling

Define `B=.75T+.25G` and `S=.6q+.4B`.

Thus `|S-B|=.6|q-B|`: interaction–bundle mismatch contracts **40%** at the support stage.

Relative to q-only:

`logit(q_full+) - logit(q_qonly+) = 1.8 d (B-q)` under the locked weights.

Secondary paired contrasts from the prospectively locked six-condition experiment:
- g20 RR direct-feedback benefit **+8.53 pp** `[+5.21,+11.85]`, RR-minus-AA benefit **+7.93 pp** `[+3.29,+12.58]`;
- g40 RR benefit **+7.80 pp** `[+4.79,+10.81]`, benefit difference **+6.33 pp** `[+1.85,+10.82]`.

Fresh prospectively locked route-duration boundary:
- AA full-minus-q-only positive-margin duration **-0.323 generations**;
- RR **-1.000**;
- RR-minus-AA DID **-0.677** `[-0.694,-0.660]`.

Take-home: recruitment buffers trait–allele mismatch; direct feedback recouples interaction toward the local bundle, but that one-step recoupling does not imply generic extension of above-switch refuge duration.

### Panel D — density-feedback failure gate

Exact q update:

`q+ = sigmoid[4.5(dq-theta)]`.

Show:
- below carrying capacity, `dq+/dN > 0`;
- smooth positive loop `q down -> N down -> density down -> q down`;
- exact headroom boundary for retaining `q+ >= .625`:

`d*q >= theta + 0.1135168053`;

- required `d*q`: **0.6160** at g1, **0.6635** at g20, **0.7135** at g40;
- deleting density -> q:
  - g20: 0/1,500 loss in both AA and RR; paired reductions **38.33 pp** and **42.53 pp**;
  - g40: reductions **57.47 pp** and **59.60 pp**.

Take-home: density feedback does not create the directional sorting advantage; it opens and amplifies the collapse regime.

## Figure 4 — Transition-exact sign can saturate while continuous last-refuge reserve predicts fate

### Panel B — even an exact route sign can saturate

Operator-balance route margin:

`M=d(0.6q+0.3T+0.1G)-(theta+0.1135168053)`.

Show:
- exact prospective audit: **1,920,000 patch-generations, 0 sign mismatches** for `sign(M)=sign(q_next-.625)`;

  - non-events 2,057/2,057 marker-positive;

Take-home: **transition-exactness does not imply fate-predictiveness**.
