## Owned claim

A scalar connectivity label does not by itself identify a transportable ecological operator. In the declared finite closure, one historical allele-frequency-mixing heterogeneity pattern failed one independent fresh-seed replication and did not port to two prospectively declared process substitutions.

## Evidence accounting

- **Independent fresh replication:** 1 — Phase U.
- **Shared-reference operator substitutions:** 2 — Phase R whole-individual dispersal and Phase S pollen-only gene flow.
- **Critical dependence:** R and S reuse exactly the same historical no-connectivity and allele-only `m=.10` blocks. They are not independent baseline replications.
- **Historical screen labels:** deterministic summaries of realized block rates, not independent conditions or covariates.

## Precision boundary

The completed five-block Pearson equal-rate tests are interpreted as precision-bounded null results, not equivalence tests.

- `N=447`: 80% power `w=0.1634039876`; approximately 8.17 pp weighted RMS near p=.5.
- `N=452`: 80% power `w=0.1624976912`; approximately 8.12 pp weighted RMS near p=.5.
- No equivalence margin was preregistered.
- No new simulation was opened for the precision audit.

## Claim ceiling

The Short Communication does **not** establish universal non-equivalence of connectivity processes, a universal connectivity scale, calibrated equality of `m`, `d` and `g`, absence of smaller heterogeneity, or natural-system validation. It reports a finite-model replication/transportability boundary only.
