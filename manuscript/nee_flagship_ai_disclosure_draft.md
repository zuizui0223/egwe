# NEE AI / automated-tool disclosure draft — author review required

Status: **draft wording only; not author-approved and not yet inserted into the submitted cover letter or manuscript.**

Policy basis: the current *Nature Ecology & Evolution* AI policy permits assistive and evaluative AI use when human accountability, verification and transparent disclosure are maintained. The journal-specific 2025 editorial additionally recommends declaring generative-AI use in the cover letter and describing AI-assisted analysis/code preparation transparently in Methods.

## 1. Cover-letter disclosure draft

> Generative-AI and automated tools were used under human supervision during manuscript drafting and editing, code drafting and review, evidence organisation, comparison of analytical alternatives, and explanation of model outputs. All scientific decisions, source verification, executable analyses, numerical results, interpretations and submitted claims remain the responsibility of the human authors and were independently checked against reproducible code, locked artifacts and source literature. AI systems were not treated as authors and did not generate primary empirical data or replace accountable scientific judgement.

## 2. Methods disclosure draft

### AI-assisted research and manuscript preparation

> Generative-AI and automated tools were used as assistive and evaluative tools for manuscript drafting and restructuring, code drafting and review, evidence organisation, comparison of analytical alternatives, and explanation of model outputs. Their outputs were not treated as data or evidence. All load-bearing numerical results were obtained from version-controlled executable analyses, exact derivations or locked workflow artifacts, and all scientific claims and interpretations were reviewed and retained under human author responsibility. AI systems were not used as authors, did not generate primary empirical data and did not replace final scholarly judgement. The exact materially used tool/model names and versions are reported in the submission metadata.

## 3. Short portal disclosure draft

> Generative-AI/automated tools assisted manuscript writing, code review, evidence organisation and analytical explanation under human supervision. Human authors verified all sources, analyses, numerical results and conclusions and retain full responsibility for the work.

## 4. Exact tool/version appendix — author verification required

Do **not** infer this list from GitHub ownership, commit messages or repository history.

Before submission, the authors should verify and insert:

- tool/provider name;
- model/product name;
- materially relevant version/date if required by the journal;
- whether the use was manuscript drafting/editing, code assistance, literature/evidence organisation, analytical comparison, or explanation of outputs;
- whether any confidential or unpublished third-party material was supplied to the tool (the submission should state only what the authors can verify).

## 5. Human-accountability checks before approval

The corresponding author should confirm that:

- every load-bearing number can be regenerated or traced to a locked artifact;
- citations were checked against source literature rather than AI summaries;
- exploratory/post-hoc analyses are labelled as such;
- no AI system is listed as an author;
- no generative-AI artwork is included as a scientific figure;
- no confidential peer-review material was supplied to an unsecured/public AI system;
- the disclosure accurately reflects the actual project history rather than minimizing use as copyediting-only.

Once author-approved, copy the relevant text into the cover letter, Methods and submission portal, replacing this draft file's review status.
