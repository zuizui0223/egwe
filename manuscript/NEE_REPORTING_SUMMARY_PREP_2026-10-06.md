## Nature form: study design

### Research design

This is a finite eco-genetic modelling study combining exact analytical derivations, prospectively locked simulation experiments, frozen warning audits, and one bounded external-consistency synthesis from an independently maintained natural-data repository.

The load-bearing finite programme comprises:

- a fixed-area fragmentation gradient;
- a matched-marginal transition-sufficiency construction;
- a six-condition relational mechanism experiment;
- a pathway edge-decomposition experiment;
- a focused q-dependent allele-sorting single-edge experiment;
- a sorting–headroom follow-up;
- an operator-balance route-duration/full-denominator experiment;
- a last-refuge warning holdout.

Natural-data results are not used to tune, fit or validate the finite closure.

### Replication

Replication was fixed before outcome access for the prospectively locked experiments.

- Fixed-area fragmentation: 1,200 attempted fresh source preparations; 1,037 met the declared high-state preparation criterion and entered the eight-patch-count projection.
- Six-condition relational mechanism experiment: 1,500 trajectories per condition with common trajectory seeds across conditions.
- Pathway edge decomposition: 5 fresh master seeds × 300 replicates = 1,500 paired keys per intervention.
- Focused allele-sorting proof: 12 fresh master seeds × 500 replicates = 6,000 paired keys per condition.
- Sorting–headroom follow-up: 12 fresh master seeds × 500 replicates × AA/RR × 2 conditions = 24,000 trajectories.
- Operator-balance route-duration test: 6 new master seeds × 500 replicates × 4 conditions = 12,000 trajectories.
- Last-refuge warning holdout: 12 new master seeds × 500 replicates × AA/RR = 12,000 trajectories.

Replication was not extended after outcomes were opened where the protocol explicitly prohibited extension.

### Randomization

No biological subjects were randomized. Simulation stochasticity is generated from predeclared seed maps. Where paired comparisons were required, the same trajectory seed was reused across the relevant conditions within each master-seed/replicate key so that contrasts were paired by construction.

### Blinding

Experimental blinding is not applicable to a deterministic/probabilistic simulation workflow without human treatment assignment or outcome assessment. Outcome access was instead controlled by prospective protocol locks: conditions, seed maps, horizons, endpoints, thresholds and intervention definitions were fixed before outcome files were opened.

### Sample-size determination

Sample sizes were fixed by the prospectively declared protocols rather than chosen after inspecting outcomes. No post-result power-based extension of simulation replication was permitted in the locked experiments. The manuscript does not claim that these finite ensemble sizes identify universal natural-system effect sizes.

### Data exclusions

The fixed-area fragmentation programme attempted 1,200 fresh source preparations; 1,037 met the predeclared high-state preparation criterion and were projected. This eligibility step is reported explicitly and is not a post-result exclusion.

For the last-refuge holdout, all 12,000 predeclared trajectories are retained in the denominator, including the 44 eventual-loss trajectories whose functional loss had already occurred by the observation snapshot.

No outcome-dependent comparator, risk-score sign flip, threshold, seed, horizon or endpoint exclusion was introduced after outcome opening.

## Nature form: statistics

### Statistical methods

The finite experiments use predeclared paired contrasts, exact algebraic identities, threshold-free ROC AUC, and confidence intervals defined in the relevant protocols.

Key inferential summaries include:

- paired difference-in-differences for intervention contrasts;
- blockwise ROC AUC for the last-refuge holdout;
- a two-sided t-based 95% confidence interval across 12 master-seed block AUCs;
- paired blockwise AUC differences for route margin versus co-timed max q;
- exact transition identities and inequality-derived thresholds where the result is algebraic rather than estimated.

The warning comparator orientation was frozen as lower state = higher future-loss risk. The directionally inverted `H_alpha` AUC was retained without post-hoc sign reversal.

Post-hoc analyses are explicitly labelled. The last-refuge component decomposition and headroom scale audit do not redefine preregistered comparator families or primary estimands.

### Multiple comparisons

No post-outcome search over alternative warning thresholds, observation times, comparator directions, horizons or intervention definitions was allowed in the locked confirmatory analyses. Secondary contrasts are identified as secondary or post hoc in the manuscript and provenance records.
