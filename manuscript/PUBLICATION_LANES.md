# Publication lanes and claim ownership

This document is the human-readable publication router. Its machine-readable counterpart is `publication_lanes.json`. The scientific four-layer decomposition remains useful for explaining the programme, but it is **not** the same thing as the current submission router.

## Active submission lane — NEE flagship

- **Submission manuscript:** `nee_flagship_article.md`
- **Target:** **Nature Ecology & Evolution**, Article.
- **Status:** `active_primary_submission`.
- **Submission rule:** **no simultaneous overlapping submission** of the state-validity or warning-validity fallback manuscripts while the flagship is under consideration.
- **Core question:** why can systems retaining the same conventional ecological/genetic marginals have different functional futures, and what representation retains information about those futures?

The flagship explicitly reuses evidence that was previously routed into two standalone EGWE manuscripts:

- state-validity layer: the constructive exact next-transition difference **0.2543** and the separately locked propagation contrasts **+5.33 pp** at generation 20 and **+5.20 pp** at generation 40;
- warning-validity layer: inherited **35/35** event leads with **48/48** non-event firings, fresh **33/33** with **49/49**, specificity **0**, binary AUC **0.5**.

It also contains load-bearing evidence that did not exist in those fallback manuscripts:

- the prospectively locked q-dependent allele-sorting single-edge DID **+6.883 pp**;
- the 24,000-trajectory sorting–headroom follow-up;
- the exact operator-balance transition audit, **1,920,000/1,920,000** patch-generations with zero sign mismatches;
- the prospectively locked continuous last-refuge holdout, AUC **0.92734** `[0.92433,0.93035]`, adding **+0.02135** AUC `[+0.01770,+0.02501]` beyond co-timed max q.

The predeclared comparator panel is reported in full. Under the fixed orientation “lower state = higher later loss risk”, co-timed `H_alpha` had AUC **0.23253**. This is a **directionally inverted** comparator result. Its sign is not flipped post hoc to manufacture a successful warning score; the inversion is retained as a boundary on the predeclared interpretation.

### Claim ceiling

All operator, route-margin, headroom and warning-performance results are bounded to the declared finite closure. Natural systems remain projection/measurement examples. The flagship does not establish a universal natural warning variable, universal eco-genetic threshold, natural causal mechanism, or portability across taxa and landscapes.

## Why the flagship is not a fifth independent paper

The old governance treated state validity and warning validity as disjoint active manuscripts and the flagship as an optional development track. That description became obsolete after the route-reserve programme produced new prospectively locked evidence and PR #165 integrated it into a complete NEE Article.

The current distinction is:

- **scientific decomposition:** EGC → state validity → warning validity → empirical measurement gate;
- **submission execution:** one active EGWE flagship that reuses state/warning evidence and adds new operator/reserve results;
- **fallbacks:** state and warning manuscripts retained intact enough to reactivate later, but not simultaneously active.

This removes the previous cover-letter contradiction without erasing the four-paper conceptual history.

- **Parallel-development rule:** development may proceed while the NEE flagship is under consideration because this lane deliberately excludes the flagship's aligned-state, operator-balance and warning/reserve claims.

## Shared scientific invariants

- Frozen endpoints, seeds, schedules, denominators and warning orientations are not retuned after outcomes.
- Parent and extension trajectories are not pooled.
- Non-significant results are not upgraded to equivalence.
- Access failures and `not_identifiable` / `not_estimable` outcomes remain visible.
- No plausible empirical proxy earns state status without endpoint-relevant predictive information.
- An inconvenient comparator result is not hidden or sign-flipped after inspection; the `H_alpha` inversion is reported as such.
- Natural systems remain at their declared resolution and are not pooled to manufacture validation.
